# 30 — Figures, attachments and terrain

Owner decisions (2026-10-17, revised 2026-10-18): original large-head figures (about 2.5 heads tall) for every operative, one body per operative archetype. v1 (M5, M9) was modular: bare-handed A-pose bodies plus weapon attachments on measured hand sockets. v2 (section 7) replaces that: every body is regenerated in a dynamic pose with its default weapons part of the mesh; only equipment (packs, belt items) may still mount on the back / belt sockets. Skeletal animation was piloted (M8: UniRig and a procedural soft-cut rig) and dropped, because the auto-rigger cannot find the hands on these fused meshes and the rigged poses looked wrong. Concepts from Gemini only. Pipeline details: `docs/knowledge-base/local-ai-studios.md`.

## 1. Style
The style sentence goes verbatim into every concept prompt (derived from the owner's reference pictures; never name a model-kit brand, use "mecha" / "robot" terms or any style label; banned prompt words (owner, 2026-10-31): see ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md 'Image prompts'): "a heroic figure, exactly two and a half heads tall, the enormous head one third of the total height and as wide as the shoulders, a short compact chest, very short thick legs with big feet, short arms with oversized hands; dense layered hard-surface detail with fine panel lines, rivets and small accents; glossy painted miniature finish, crisp edges, saturated colours." Framing: whole figure on a plain round black base, studio product photo, three-quarter view from slightly below, pure white background, no text, no logos, no emblems. Two dominant hues per team (for the hue-band army painter): Angels of Death deep blue / gold; Kommandos green skin and black-red scrap. Check proportions in the image, not the prompt; reject extra limbs/weapons, fused parts, text, cropped bases.

## 2. Body contract (`public/assets/models/bodies/<archetype>.glb`)
- Origin at base centre, y up, 1 unit = 1 inch, front +Z, pre-scaled to the datacard height (`heightIn` in the manifest), black base disc added upstream by `stage_finalize.py`.
- **Weapons in the body (v2)**: the concept shows the operative holding its default weapons in a dynamic pose and Hunyuan fuses them into one mesh. Sockets are measured after generation by `tools/models/measure-sockets.py` (back, beltR/L, shoulderR/L, head; hand sockets only on the remaining v1 bodies) and stored in the manifest, not in the GLB.
- Manifest `public/assets/models/manifest.json`: `{ bodies: { <slug>: { file, archetype, heightIn, figureHeightIn, baseTopIn, baseMm, measured, sockets: { handR?, handL?, back, beltR, beltL, shoulderL, shoulderR, head }, weaponsBaked?, variants?: string[] } }, attachments: { <weaponOrEquipId>: { file, holdClass: pistol|rifle|long|heavy|melee1h|melee2h|shield|pack|grenade|tool, grip: Vec3, support?: Vec3, scale } } }`.
- `weaponsBaked` is written by the socket tool for every slug listed in `tools/local-gen/v2_units.json`; `variants` comes from its `VARIANTS` table.
- Pose variants: a second (sometimes third) body for an archetype a team fields several times (`<archetype>-kneeling`, `-b`, `-c`), so a squad does not repeat one pose. Weapon-option bodies only where the option changes the silhouette (decided per team in `art/unit-concepts/PROMPTS.md`); pistol or blade swaps keep the default body.

## 3. Attachments (`public/assets/models/attachments/<id>.glb`)
One GLB per weapon or visible equipment: bolt rifle, auto bolt rifle, stalker bolt rifle, heavy bolt pistol, plasma pistol, hand flamer, chainsword, power fist, power weapon, thunder hammer; slugga, choppa, big choppa, power klaw, burna, rokkit launcha, breacha ram, snipa, dakka shoota, grot-related bits; grenades (belt), comms pack (back), medi-pack… Concept: side view, horizontal, barrel pointing right, no hands, white background. Scale: weapon length in inches relative to the body height (data in manifest). Attachment GLBs are also fused Hunyuan meshes, flat-tinted when paint fails.

## 4. Rendering (`src/client/figures/`)
`Operative.tsx` picks the body with `pickBody` (archetype, then one of its `variants` by a hash of the operative id, so a squad spreads its poses), mounts weapon attachments at the hand sockets only for bodies without `weaponsBaked` (v1 leftovers; per-class offset/rotation in the `HOLDS` table), applies the team paint shader (hue-band remap of the two source hues), and plays rigid whole-body cues (idle breathe, walk bob, shoot recoil, melee lunge, hit flinch, four death styles). Skinned animation is out (M8 dropped). Procedural stand-in (`Procedural.tsx`: capsule body, box weapon by hold class, coloured by team) while a GLB is missing or loading. `?gallery` shows every body × pose × attachment for the QC pass.

## 5. Terrain
Volkus-style set: stronghold, large ruin (two floors with vantage), small ruin, heavy rubble, light rubble, barricades, containers, pipes; each piece a GLB fitted to its rules footprint (`terrainFit.ts`) with traits per part from the killzone layout data. Concepts via Gemini ("fully painted tabletop wargame terrain piece, grimdark ruined gothic-industrial, white background"), Hunyuan, decimated to about 6k triangles; flats (rubble patches, floor plates) procedural. Vantage floors are explicit placeable surfaces in data so the engine and the renderer agree on heights.

## 6. Generation plan (v1, M5; section 7 supersedes it for bodies)
1. One Gemini chat per team: a clean base figure (sergeant), then edit-variants for each archetype and pose; one chat for weapons/equipment side views; one for terrain.
2. `units.json`/`picks.json` entries on both GPU machines (this PC and Alteisen via `ssh alteisen`, `E:\AI\py2.bat`/`py21.bat`), batches of ≤9 per machine, one GPU job per machine.
3. `stage_finalize.py` → `public/assets/models/...`, `tools/models/optimize.mjs` (meshopt; loader has `setMeshoptDecoder`), sockets measured, manifest updated, `?gallery` contact sheets sent as FYI.

## 7. v2 bodies: dynamic poses, weapons in the mesh (2026-10-18)
Gallery check so far: AoD and Kommando v2 bodies face +Z, poses read, weapons attached; the feet-based facing vote in `measure-sockets.py` is unreliable on dynamic poses (ignore the `?`).
Owner decisions: A-poses look generic, so every body is regenerated in a role pose (leader pointing, rifleman aiming, kneeling sniper, heavy gunner braced, melee charge, caster, support crouched, grenadier throwing, beast or drone in motion) holding its default weapons; archetypes a team fields several times get pose variants; all ten teams; generation on Alteisen, with the Gemini image quota as the pacing limit (prompt the next team while the previous one renders).
1. Concepts: one Gemini chat per team (Claude in Chrome), one standalone prompt per body: subject and gear, weapons and which hand holds them, the pose line, the section 1 style sentence, framing. Prompt lists live in `%USERPROFILE%\hf-tools\<team>-prompts-gemini.json`; every prompt is logged in `art/unit-concepts/PROMPTS.md` (v2 section); images land in `art/unit-concepts/v2/<slug>.png` with a contact sheet per team. Export: an in-page JSON bundle of the chat's images (canvas to data URL), one download, decoded by python. Gemini refuses or skips a few per chat: resend with reworded gear.
2. Bodies: concepts to `E:\AI\work\mancermundo\v2\concepts\` on Alteisen, picks `tools/local-gen/v2_picks_<team>.alteisen.json`, units `tools/local-gen/v2_units.json`, `tools/local-gen/run_v2_alteisen.bat <picks> <slugs...>` (cutout, shape, paint, finalize into `E:\AI\work\mancermundo\v2\bodies`), queued per team through `schtasks` wrappers that wait for the previous team's GLBs. About 2 min shape and 4.5 min paint per body on the RTX 3080.
3. Back here: GLBs replace the v1 files in `public/assets/models/bodies/` (same slugs; variants are new slugs), `measure-sockets.py` rewrites the manifest (`weaponsBaked`, `variants`), `?gallery` check per team (facing, pose, nothing cropped), commit per team, push.
4. Hugging Face providers were tested as a concept source (FLUX.1-dev, Qwen-Image through `tools/art/hf-concepts.py`): FLUX ignores the head ratio, Qwen gets the proportions but paints chapter badges and grey backdrops. Gemini stays the source.
