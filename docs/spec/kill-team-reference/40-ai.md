# 40 — AI opponent

The AI is the product: Kill Team has no official solo mode. One `Decider` implementation per tier, all in `src/ai/`, running in a Web Worker in the client (`src/ai/worker.ts`, `src/client/bot/aiWorkerClient.ts`) and inline in `tools/sim.ts` and `tools/ai-bench.ts`. The AI sees only `view(state, seat)` and answers only from `legalActions` (or validated continuous answers); the host re-validates every answer and falls back to the random decider.

## 1. Tiers
| Tier | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `random` | uniform over legal actions; move paths = random legal point within range (test instrument and bench baseline) |
| `easy` | utility scoring with noise (random among the top 3) and no lookahead |
| `normal` | utility scoring, one-activation lookahead for shoot/charge outcomes, tac-aware objective play |
Difficulty is scoring noise plus lookahead depth; tiers are env-tunable (`AI_TUNE`), per-decision time budget `AI_MS` (default 150 ms) mapped to a deterministic work cap with a wall-clock hard stop.

## 2. Scoring model (`src/ai/eval.ts`, `threat.ts`, `moves.ts`)
- **Expected damage** from `engine.query.expectedDamage(attacker, weapon, target)` (exact dice math over Atk/Hit/Dmg vs Save with cover save, obscured, Piercing, Lethal, Rending etc.), never re-derived in the AI.
- **Threat map**: for each candidate position, the sum over enemy ready operatives of expected damage they could inflict next activation given visibility, cover and order; concealed-in-cover positions score as untargetable.
- **Objective value**: contest/control of markers weighted by the crit op (Secure: hold at end of turning point; Loot: adjacency with AP to spare; Transmission: hold), by turning point, and by the kill op grade race.
- **Activation planner**: for the activated operative enumerate candidate plans (order × move target × action list within APL) and score `damageDealt − damageTakenRisk + objectiveDelta + positional`; pick the best, with the easy tier's noise.
- **Activation order**: pick the ready operative whose best plan has the highest score; counteract when it yields value.
- **Ploys**: scored by expected effect (Command Re-roll when a single die flips the outcome; team ploys via per-ploy heuristics in `src/ai/ploys.ts`).
- **Fight**: strike/block alternation solved by a small minimax over remaining dice (both sides' Normal/Crit Dmg, wounds), which also powers the human-side `auto` block option.
- **Setup**: deploy by thirds into cover-rich spots near objectives with Conceal; equipment markers in the own territory where they matter.

## 3. Bench (`tools/ai-bench.ts`)
Mirror matches on a fixed layout and mission set, both teams, both seats; count decisive results. Gates: normal beats random ≥ 95% over 40 games; normal ≥ easy; mean decision time < 50 ms; zero rejections, zero stalls, zero fallbacks to random. Also audits behaviour: share of activations with zero actions (must be < 2%), concealed operatives left in the open, operatives idle on objectives.

## 4. Non-goals for v1
No Monte Carlo tree search, no multi-turn lookahead, no learned models. Later: hard tier with 2-activation lookahead and opponent modelling.
