# v2 figure concepts (role pose, default weapons fused into the figure)

Made in Google Gemini (Flash, image generation) through Claude in Chrome, one chat per house (a new tab each, closed afterwards), one standalone prompt per figure, 1024x572 PNGs exported from the page (canvas to data-URL bundle, decoded with `tools/art/decode_bundle.py`; a few images arrive as cross-origin lh3 URLs and were exported from their own fresh tab). Prompts are built by `tools/art/v2_prompts.py` (subject and gear, which hand holds what, pose line, proportion sentence, framing), which also writes `prompts-gemini.json` and `figures.json`. Contact sheets: `contact-goliath.png`, `contact-escher.png` (`tools/art/contact_sheet.py`).

On-style reference (Murder Squad approved v2 concept): `art/unit-concepts/v2/ks-sergeant-bolt-pistol.png` inside the `next-kill-teams-9efd74` worktree of the `wh40k-murder-squad` project (head about 40% of the figure height including base, as wide as the shoulders).

## Generation budget
30 of 34 generations used: 27 picks plus 3 rejects (all three on the first Goliath figure). Gemini never refused or hit a quota; no Flux/SDXL substitutes were used.

## Style sentence notes
The Murder Squad sentence ("head one third of the height" / "about 40%") produced Goliath heads at about 26-31% of the height (brutes with shoulder plates read too small-headed). The final wording overstates the head on purpose ("bobblehead proportions ... gigantic round head ... about 50% ... as wide as the shoulders"), which measures about 34% of total height including the base (38% without), matching the reference. The same sentence went into all 27 figure prompts. No model-kit brand words, "mecha", "chibi" or "Gundam" are used (checked by script). Gemini sometimes swaps left and right: those images were mirrored in post (noted per figure). Sidearms are shown holstered where both hands are full. 13-gangs.md gives no larger base for Goliath leaders or champions, so every base is 25 mm in `figures.json`.

## Rejects (first Goliath figure, `goliath-forge-tyrant-chainaxe`, saved in `rejects/`)

1. reject1: first prompt, head about 26% of height, about 3.8 heads tall, huge pauldrons.
2. reject2: shoulder plates toned down, "giant oversized head about 40%": head about 24-28%, still too small.
3. reject3: "gigantic round head almost half of the total height (about 45%)": head about 31%, closer but short of 35-40%.
4. Pick: "bobblehead proportions ... about 50%": head about 34% with base, as wide as the shoulders. All later figures used this wording and passed first time.

Attempts 1-3 used the same subject, gear and pose text as `goliath-forge-tyrant-chainaxe` below (attempt 1 had "enormous bolted dark-bronze iron pauldrons" and "hulking" in the subject) with these proportion sentences:
- 1: "Proportions: a super-deformed heroic figure, exactly two and a half heads tall, the enormous head about 40% of the total height and as wide as the shoulders, a short compact chest, chunky limbs, very short thick legs with big feet, short arms with oversized hands; ..."
- 2: "... with a giant oversized head, exactly two and a half heads tall, the enormous head about 40% of the total height, taller than the torso, and as wide as the shoulders, a very short compact chest, ..."
- 3: "... with a gigantic round head, exactly two and a half heads tall, the enormous head almost half of the total height (about 45%), wider than the torso and as wide as the shoulders, a tiny very short compact chest, ..."
